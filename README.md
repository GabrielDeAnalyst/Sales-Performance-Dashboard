# Sales-Performance-Dashboard
Interactive Power BI sales report analyzing $80.45M in sales amount, $79.98M total product cost, $6.44M tax and $27.05M summed unit price. Bikes lead sales at $66M, ahead of Components ($12M), Clothing ($2M) and Accessories ($1M). Visuals cover territory, region and reseller, with slicers for category, subcategory, product, country and region.
